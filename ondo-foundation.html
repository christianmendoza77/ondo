Ondo Perps

Perpetual futures on stocks, ETFs, and commodities, with tokenized equities as collateral.

  • Tokenized stocks and ETFs as collateral
  • Portfolio margin across multiple equities
  • Up to 20x leverage, traded 24/7
  • Deep liquidity from institutional market makers
  • Non-custodial — you hold your collateral

Ondo Stocks

A platform bringing exposure to traditional public securities onchain, usable in DeFi.

  • Hundreds of US stocks and ETFs, onchain
  • Instant minting and redemption, 24/5 (24/7 on select assets)
  • Freely transferable on Ethereum, BNB Chain, and Solana
  • Total-return exposure with dividends reinvested
  • Daily third-party verification of asset backing

USDY

A permissionless yieldcoin: the utility of stablecoins, with safer daily yield.

  • Secured by short-term US Treasuries and bank deposits
  • Yield accrues daily, whether held, staked, or pledged
  • Freely transferable and composable in DeFi
  • Daily subscriptions and redemptions
  • Debt issued by a bankruptcy-remote entity
